## About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at [ETH Zurich](https://ai-econ-lab.org) under the supervision of [Elliott Ash](https://elliottash.com) and associated researcher at the [ETH AI Center](https://ai.ethz.ch).

I work mostly on topics in empirical IO, urban economics, and the economics of AI.

I co-organize the [Online Seminar in Economics + Data Science](https://econ-ds-seminar.github.io). You can [join our mailing list](https://sympa.ethz.ch/sympa/subscribe/econ.data.sci.online.seminar?previous_action=info) to stay up to date with our events.


[See my CV](/assets/docs/cv.pdf) for more information.
